How this works. The directory is generated automatically from publication records in OpenAlex. A paper counts as animal welfare research if its title or abstract matches welfare-related terms: welfare phrases, "welfare" together with an animal, or welfare indicators in papers OpenAlex classifies under a welfare topic. Institutions and researchers are taken from the authors' affiliations on those papers. Green tags show research fields (types of welfare indicators studied), grey tags show species; a tag appears when it covers at least 5% of the papers. "Papers" = number of matching papers since 2010.

A researcher's latest affiliation is the institution on their most recent paper in the directory, so it may lag behind a recent move. Researchers whose most recent paper is from elsewhere are listed under an institution as earlier members.

Because this is automated, some entries will be incomplete or wrong (e.g. outdated affiliations or merged names).
